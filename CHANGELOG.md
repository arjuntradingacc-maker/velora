# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (versionCode 1)

Welcome to Velora — a password manager built to keep your vault private.

- Encrypted local vault for passwords, passkeys, cards, notes, Wi-Fi, codes & more
- Built-in strong password generator
- Security Center with privacy-preserving breach checks
- Biometric unlock, Autofill for other apps
- Scan a card with your camera to fill it in automatically
- Light & dark themes, home-screen widgets

No ads. No tracking. Your data stays yours.
