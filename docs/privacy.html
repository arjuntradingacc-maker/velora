Velora Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Velora ("the app") is a password and credential manager for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles, what leaves your device, and what never does.

The short version

  • Your vault (passwords, passkeys, notes, cards, and other saved items) is encrypted on your device and unlocked only by your master password, PIN, or biometric.
  • We do not run analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs of any kind. We do not sell or share your data with third parties, ever.
  • The only things that ever leave your device are described explicitly below — and even then, never in a form we can read.

What's stored on your device

Every vault item you save — logins, passkeys, secure notes, payment cards, personal info, Wi-Fi credentials, one-time codes, documents, API keys, and recovery codes — is stored in an encrypted local database. The encryption key is generated on your device and held in the Android Keystore (using hardware-backed storage where your device supports it), gated behind your master password, PIN, or biometric unlock. We do not have a copy of this key and cannot decrypt your vault ourselves.

The app does not use Android's automatic cloud backup for this data, so your vault is not included in device backups.

What can leave your device, and why

1. Optional cross-device sync

If you turn sync on, each vault record is encrypted on your device before it is sent, along with a randomly generated device identifier used only to tell your devices apart. The sync service stores and relays encrypted blobs — it cannot read their contents.

2. Breach monitoring

If you use the Security Center's breach check, the app never sends your password or username anywhere. Instead, it hashes each password on your device and sends only the first 5 characters of that hash to check it against a public breach-index range (the same privacy-preserving technique used by Have I Been Pwned). There is no way to reconstruct your password from what's sent.

3. Card scanning

The "scan card" feature uses your camera and on-device text recognition to read a card number, expiry, and name so you don't have to type them. The photo is processed in memory and is never saved to your device's gallery, never uploaded, and is discarded immediately after the fields are filled in.

4. Biometric unlock

Fingerprint or face unlock is handled entirely by Android's own biometric system. The app never receives or stores your raw biometric data — only a yes/no authentication result from the operating system.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad SDKs, no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing your data with third parties.
  • No access to your vault contents by us — it's encrypted before it ever reaches a network call, and we don't hold the keys.

Permissions

  • Camera — only used when you choose to scan a card or a QR/setup code.
  • Biometric hardware — only used to unlock your vault.
  • Notifications — only used for local security alerts (e.g. a breach was found).

Your controls

  • You can export or delete any vault item at any time from within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored vault data from your device.
  • If you've used sync, contact us (below) to request deletion of synced data from the server.
